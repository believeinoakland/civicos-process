# neoProcess — read this first

This repository defines how the product is built (P2); it holds no product code. The principles are `PROCESS-DESIGN.md`. The rules are the ten rulebooks in `rulebooks/` (one screen each) and the design files beside this one: `PROTOCOL.md` (how sessions reach each other), `JOB-LIFE.md` (a job as a pull request), `SESSIONS.md` (models, reading sets, refreshes), `DESIGN-STAGE.md`, `PERMISSIONS.md`, `PRODUCT-SUPPLIES.md`, `SETUP-STEPS.md`; `PROCESS-MECHANICS.md` is the index of which text governs what. Principle P17 sets the working relationship with Bob at all times; a principle changes only with his approval.

Start every session by invoking the skill `neoprocess` with your role (`/neoprocess bob`, `job`, `lane`, `root`, or `process` for the neoProcess lane that maintains this repository): it prints your process text, which you read whole and never scan (P20), and the start command (`proc takeover`, `proc job start`, `proc lane start`, `proc process start`) that records your role; the hooks refuse every act until it is recorded.

Changes here are process changes (P3): checked against the principles and proven on a dry-run tranche (`dryrun/`; the certification `dryrun/N1/CERTIFICATION.md`) before product work relies on them. The suite: `node --test checks/test/*.test.mjs tools/test/*.test.mjs`. The product is `believeinoakland/bio` (to be `civicsmith`); its `build/manifest.md` says where its build state lives.
