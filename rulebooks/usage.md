# USE · Usage · what sessions cost, what Bob's meter says, and moving between accounts

In force from 2026-10-29, certified on N1. At most ten rules, one screen, each checkable. Tags: YOURS (Bob's policy) or BOB'S; CODE names what enforces it, TEXT means nothing does.

1. A session's usage is its tokens processed (cache reads, cache writes, input, output) as `archive_session` returns them, tagged by tranche; no dollar estimate and no session's own count is used. (P14) BOB'S · CODE: `proc job archived`, `proc bob archived` take the archive's four counts and write the row.
2. Every archived session, a failed one included, gets one row keyed by its cloud session id, a BOB's written by its successor under the tranche it worked in; a session that cannot be read (left on another account, or a job whose archive is refused, issue 95) gets no counts and is named unmeasured, never estimated. (P14, P20; K742) BOB'S · CODE: `proc bob archived`, `proc job archived` (`--unmeasured`); the rest TEXT.
3. A job's row carries its size, the last `Size` line of its record (test runs, module lines), so tokens can be set against size. (P14) BOB'S · CODE: `proc job complete` writes the line; `proc job archived` copies it.
4. No tranche, job or session is bounded by usage until a model of cost exists; after a few tranches BOB reports the model (tokens per job against size, tokens against the meter, BOB's cost against its context) and Bob sets and approves any bound. Meanwhile Bob sees each tranche's measured usage and footprint when it closes. (P14, P17) YOURS · CODE: `proc footprint`; the rest TEXT.
5. Bob's weekly meter is the authoritative measure. BOB notes it when Bob gives it, at a tranche's open and close, and never asks for it or raises it or a platform usage warning. (P14, P17; Bob, K5136) YOURS · TEXT.
6. No layer, job or tranche waits on the meter: development runs until Bob says pause or stop. (P19; Bob, K2605, K4614, K5136) YOURS · TEXT.
7. No job-count cap applies, except that `proc layer start` starts nothing while `rate_limit_info.status` is not `allowed`; BOB then tells Bob in one line. (P14; Bob, K5323) YOURS · CODE: `proc layer start --usage`; the telling is TEXT.
8. Every session spends tokens only on what its step needs: no speculative worker, no repeated walk, bulk reading to a worker on the Sessions table's model and effort. (P14; Bob, K3549, K4614) YOURS · TEXT.
9. An account move starts only on Bob's word. BOB then writes `MOVING` and the target account in the plan's Status line and pushes; Bob starts a ROOT on the new account, his one act, walked through in plain steps; that ROOT starts the next BOB. (P13, P17; Bob, K5136, K4790; PROTOCOL §7a) YOURS · TEXT.
10. In a move every party is replaced from git on the new account, BOB first, then each lane and job from its record; an old session is archived once its successor's address is in git, and its row is written; `build/channels.md`'s BOB row follows the account (`xwriter --as BOB` on the new account). (P13, P14, P15; PROTOCOL §7a, §7d) BOB'S · CODE: `proc epoch close` refuses while a session is unarchived or an ask is open.
