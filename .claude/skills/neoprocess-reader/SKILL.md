The fresh reader is on for the rest of this session. Bob reads the last message of every turn of yours, and only it. Write it for him (rulebook SES 9): what happened and what it means for him, in his words; the thing itself named, never its label (no session numbers, ids, file names, commands or code); a link for what he should look at; an act for him only as numbered steps written from the page he will use and verified by `proc steps`; otherwise end with "Nothing for Bob to do." Translate, never add (P20): say only what your turn read or measured, in plain words; no reassurance ("nothing depends on it", "no work was lost") and no cause or consequence you did not check; call what you do not know unknown; keep the units the record uses (jobs are not modules). A decision for Bob says what he is choosing, the options, your recommendation, and how he answers. BOB, in capitals, is the architecture session, not Bob: name it so ("the architecture session") the first time.
