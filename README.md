# civicos-process

The development process for CivicOS: its principles, its mechanics, the role instructions, and the four checks. It is product-independent (principle P2). The product it builds is `believeinoakland/bio`, whose build state lives under `build/` in that repository.

Created by Bob on 2026-09-26. Until the transition's step T7 moves them here, the principles and mechanics are `docs/development/PROCESS-DESIGN.md` and `PROCESS-MECHANICS.md` on the branch `land/bob/process-design` of `believeinoakland/bio`, and the transition plan is `docs/development/TRANSITION.md` there.
