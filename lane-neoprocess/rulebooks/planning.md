# PLN · Planning · plans written from the work that remains, placed at once, prepared ahead

Area rulebook, trial draft (neoProcess #3, 2026-10-29). At most ten rules, one screen, each checkable. Tags: YOURS (Bob's policy) or BOB'S; CODE names what enforces it, TEXT means nothing does.

1. A plan is written from the work that remains, not from the work that is queued: it carries every entry that can safely be done in the tranche, with no cap on jobs, and names the hard reason for each entry it leaves out. (P19; Bob, K642, K889) YOURS · CODE: the format check fails a deferring phrase in `current.md` or the handoff that does not cite `next.md`; TEXT otherwise.
2. An entry is left out for one of three reasons only: a decision of Bob's not yet made; a deployment or measurement that cannot happen inside the tranche; something outside the product that does not yet exist. Never the order, a closed layer, the tranche's size, a job's smallness or no change in behaviour. (P19; Bob, K3915) YOURS · CODE: the format check fails a `next.md` entry added while a tranche runs with any other reason.
3. Work found while a round runs is placed at once: in the running job of its module; else in the START of its module's job in a layer not yet started; else in the completion round. Nothing found waits for the next tranche; at each takeover BOB starts any item left for later that has none of the three reasons. (P10, P19; Bob, K3915; rulings-active §1) YOURS · TEXT.
4. A change to a provided service is written into the provider's requirements on the tranche branch, and every module that uses it gets an entry in the same tranche, placed by rule 3. (P5, P10) BOB'S · TEXT.
5. A round's plan, entries grouped by module under their layers, is copied whole by `proc round open`, which refuses while another round is open; a running entry's meaning is not changed, and entries may still join a layer not yet started. No tranche opens while an archived plan reads CLOSING and `main` is behind it, or while the words check reports a finding. (P10, P12; Bob, K882) BOB'S · CODE: `proc round open`; TEXT for the rest.
6. Layers run lowest first; all jobs of a layer start together and the next layer starts only when the layer has closed. A job outside its layer's turn needs Bob's approval each time. (P10; Bob, K646, K1548) YOURS · CODE: `proc layer start`, `proc layer close`.
7. The completion round runs after the last layer and before the release: one job per closed-layer module with found work, all concurrent, lower layers first where one uses another. It needs no further approval and is the tranche's last round. (P10; Bob, K3915, K3923) YOURS · TEXT.
8. Every plan opens with its legacy census: each legacy module in `modules.json` with the entry that retires it or the hard reason it stays; opening a tranche, BOB tells Bob in one line what it covers. (P19, P17) BOB'S · TEXT.
9. While a tranche runs BOB prepares what follows, on the tranche branch: the next plan, the requirements of coming modules (Bob's where they are his), the extraction maps. It never touches `main`, a running job's module or requirements a running job reads, and never idles while work remains. (P18, P19) BOB'S · CODE for `main` (BOB's hook refuses the push); TEXT otherwise.
10. A tranche's report to Bob states its elapsed time, the work finished (legacy lines removed, entries applied), each deferred entry with its reason, the work left and its cost. (P19, P14; Bob, K646) YOURS · TEXT.
