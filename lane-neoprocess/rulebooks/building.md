# BLD · Building · a job's round on one module: read whole, change, prove at the interface, report

Area rulebook, trial draft (neoProcess #3, 2026-10-29). At most ten rules, one screen, each checkable. Tags: YOURS (Bob's policy) or BOB'S; CODE names what enforces it, TEXT means nothing does.

1. A job reads its set whole: its START, its module's requirements (both parts), its entries, its layer's contract, its code, of each used module the services its entries touch, and the tests naming a requirement its entries change; its other tests are run, not read. A read that stops short, or a set document over its limit, stops the job at `BLOCKED: reading`. (P20, P6; Bob K3271, K3588, K3678) YOURS · CODE: the PreToolUse hook; `proc layer start` and `job start` measure the set against 40 KB.
2. An ambiguity, or a flaw in another module or in the requirements, goes to BOB as a `QUESTION` or `REPORT` in the requirement's terms, with the job's evidence; the job writes `WAITING:` in its record, pushes, then sends, and carries on against its best reading unless the answer decides what it builds next. (P9, P20) BOB'S · CODE: the hook allows `send_message` only to BOB's address and only with the record pushed.
3. A flaw or improvement in the job's own module is dealt with in the job; one it cannot finish goes to BOB. (P8, P10) BOB'S · TEXT.
4. A job writes only its module's `paths` and `tests` and the strike of its `**Met**` marks in the requirements file; its record is its pull request and its state line, written by the tool; it never commits a generated artifact. (P7, P12; JOB-LIFE §2, §3) BOB'S · CODE: the ownership check; the gate regenerates artifacts for its run and restores them.
5. Every requirement id is named by a test at the module's interface that checks full compliance with it, never its source text; no test hand-builds another module's answer or words. (P7, P11; Bob K4990, K4993) YOURS · CODE: the coverage, words and fixtures checks; the cannot-fail check (planned).
6. A job that changes a provided service also runs its users' tests, once BOB has updated the requirements. (P11) BOB'S · CODE: the gate runs the tests of every module that uses the module.
7. A test or check red that the tranche already had is inherited, named in the report and not owned; a new red refuses completion. (P7, P19) BOB'S · CODE: `proc job complete` and the gate compare with the measure before the work.
8. A report carries a `**Met**` line and an `**Evidence**` section of at least one item (a command with its result line, a file:line, or a test run with its totals). (P20, P7) BOB'S · CODE: `proc job complete` refuses without them.
9. After three honest attempts a red test is `BLOCKED: tests`, worked with BOB, who may clarify the requirement, route the cause or re-scope the entry; only then is the entry deferred. (P8, P19) BOB'S · TEXT.
10. A job ends a turn only on COMPLETE or a named wait, with everything pushed, and stays available until retired: a `CHANGE` re-opens it on a fresh branch and it completes again. (P13, P10) BOB'S · CODE: the Stop hook; `proc job reopen`.
