# GOV · Governance · who decides what; how a rule is made, changed and retired

Area rulebook, trial draft (neoProcess #3, 2026-10-29). At most ten rules, one screen, each checkable. Tags: YOURS (Bob's policy) or BOB'S; CODE names what enforces it, TEXT means nothing does.

1. Bob decides only policy and doctrine, what a requirement means, the high-level architecture (layers; modules that carry product capability) and UX. Every lower-level matter is BOB's: decided without asking, recorded once, reported to Bob as done. (P17; K2055, K1257) YOURS · TEXT.
2. A question to Bob comes with an explanation he can follow, options and a recommendation, and only after BOB checks he has not already answered it. When unsure whose a question is, BOB decides by P17 and says which way it read it. (P17; K1913, K2009) YOURS · TEXT.
3. A principle changes only after Bob has seen the exact change, rendered, and approved it in words; an answer to another question is not approval. (P16; K1258) YOURS · TEXT.
4. Any other process text (mechanics, tools, rulebooks) is BOB's to change. Every rule names the principle it serves, and none is added in reaction to an incident unless a principle yields it. (P16; K5036, K113) YOURS · TEXT.
5. A process change is certified before product work uses it: checked against the principles, then proven on a dry-run tranche, each path in the certification list proven or named as provable only in a product run, with the check that will prove it. A meaningful flaw stops product work until then. (P3; mechanics §15; K111) BOB'S · TEXT.
6. A lane's decision on a rule is drafted by the lane; BOB rewrites the rulebook in place, and Bob sees before and after if the rule is his. It is written in its home before the requester is answered. (P15; DESIGN-STAGE §1, §2) BOB'S · CODE: `proc ask close` refuses a reply whose files are not in the same commit.
7. A ruling is recorded once, one line `K<n> · date · area · ruling · why`, and is changed only by a new line naming its evidence and the line it replaces; it is not reopened without new evidence named in writing. A rule cites only the latest decision that sets it. (P15; mechanics §9; K3676) BOB'S · CODE: `proc ruling` numbers it; the format check refuses an id given twice.
8. A ruling of Bob's quotes his words and adds no limit he did not state; BOB's additions are marked "BOB's details (P17)"; where they differ, his words govern. (P15; K1762) YOURS · TEXT.
9. A refused act is never worked around. The session records `NEEDS BOB: <the one act>`; BOB tells Bob in one line and never approves for him; the ruling that records his approval states its standing-list decision. Before an act on `main` or another party's branch BOB searches the log for a refusal. (P17; mechanics §16) YOURS · CODE: `proc status` lists `RELAY TO BOB`; the format check fails an approval with no standing-list decision.
10. Merges and every other repository act are BOB's and never asked of Bob; the one development act he takes is approving a GitHub release. (P17; K5346) YOURS · TEXT.
