# Situation: starting a layer; a job's session ends or fills (P8, P10, P13)

- **Start:** read `rate_limit_info.status` from `get_session` (yours), then `proc layer start --layer <L> --usage <status>`. It pushes each job's branch, writes each START, and prints one call per job: `message` (the module's session from an earlier round is live: send the START to it by `send_message`, then `proc job messaged --module <m>`) or `create_session` (call it exactly as printed, then `proc job started --module <m> --name "<TITLE>" --session <new id>`). The create_session hook refuses any other call. Model: the cheapest able (haiku for test-only, wording-only and mechanical re-points; sonnet otherwise), passed as `model`.
- **A job's session stays active until its layer closes** (P10): its merge does not end it; a later CHANGE goes to it by message.
- **Before messaging a job,** `proc job route --module <m> [--session-status <get_session status>] [--context-pct <n>]`: it prints `message` to its live session, or `create_session` for a fresh one when that session is archived, failed, or past half its window (archive the old one first and write its row with `proc job archived`).
- **A job that went quiet** (`proc status` QUIET; or a child-session event that its turn failed): read it with `get_session`; idle or failed while its record reads WORKING, route a message ("continue from your record"); still nothing at the next check, start a fresh session by `proc job route`.
- **The next layer starts the moment the last closes** (P19, bio K5722): a layer open and no job live is never a place to end a turn; the Stop hook refuses it unless `proc bob waiting` names the one act only Bob can take.
