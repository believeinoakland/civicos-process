# The act table: retired

Retired on 30 Oct 2026 by PLATFORM #1 (tranche N2, entry N2-1; REQUIREMENTS PLT-1, PLT-2). It listed every act with the result measured on the dry run, and tried to predict the auto-mode classifier, which decides some acts case by case. Its rows are folded into the platform module, `tools/platform.mjs`, as data: the refusals and limits are its constraints, each with the day it was last confirmed, its count, its evidence and the workaround; the acts that pass are its acts, with their pass counts. The rendering is `tools/platform.md`. A refusal recorded by `proc refused --act <id>` reads as predicted when the id names a row there: exactly, by its call name, or by an alias for an id of this table (`isKnown`). The table as it last stood is in git history (`git show e277c2a:tools/acts.md`).
