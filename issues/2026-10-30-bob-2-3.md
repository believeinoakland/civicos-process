# No second review round: a job whose review found gaps cannot be reviewed again or merged after its rework (review decide, review result and merge refuse)

**The flaw.** The tool has no second review round. `proc review decide` refuses once a review is decided (`<m>'s review is decided: …`), `proc review result` refuses unless the review line reads `working since`, and `proc merge` refuses a review line that reads `gaps: n` (`not approved`). So a job whose review found gaps, fixed them and completed again cannot be reviewed again nor merged; `proc job reopen` is for a merged job only.

**Measured.** 30 Oct 2026, tranche N2 layer 1: both reviews read gaps (platform 7, record 18; `jobs/<m>/review.md` on `state/E1`); the platform job's rework passed the gate at 21:16:59Z (run 41129824505). Read in `tools/proc.mjs`: `reviewDecide` (`if (j.review) refuse(...)`), `reviewResult` (`if (!/^review · .* · working since /.test(j.review)) refuse`), `merge` (`if (/^review · /.test(j.review) && !/ · approved · /.test(j.review)) refuse`).

**Workaround in force (K9).** On the job's next completion the architecture session resets the job line's review field to `review · <reason> · <note> · working since <now>` by the tool's own job-line functions, dispatches the reviewer from the recorded brief again (`jobs/<m>/review-brief.md`, the same brief, `reviews` counted up), and records the verdict with `proc review result` as usual; the pull request carries both reviews.

**Fix route.** Integration's entry N2-27 (`tools/integration.mjs`): `review decide` on a COMPLETE job whose last review read gaps opens the next round (reviews + 1, the brief reused unless given), and `merge` reads the latest round.
